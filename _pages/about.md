I’m a PhD student in [Digital Media](https://dm.lmc.gatech.edu/) at Georgia Institute of Technology under [Dr. Yanni Loukissas](https://loukissas.lmc.gatech.edu/) in the [Interdisciplinary Media Arts Center](https://media-arts.gatech.edu/center). I also work with [Dr. Richmond Wong](https://richmondywong.com/), in the [Creating Ethics Infrastructures Lab](https://ethicsinfrastructures.lmc.gatech.edu/), and previously with [Dr. Mona Slone](https://www.monasloane.org/), in the [Sloane Lab](https://www.monasloane.org/sloane-lab).

I work in the fields of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Science and Technology Studies (STS), and Design. My research explores the reciprocal relationship between humans and technology: how technologies shape social practices, values, and ways of being, and how human choices, cultures, and imaginaries in turn shape the design and use of technologies. Through this work, I aim to reimagine more meaningful and responsible technological futures.

Previously, I studied Human Centered Design and Engineering (HCDE) at the University of Washington and Integrated Design and Media (IDM) at New York University.

**Ploy Pruekcharoen**, Dylan McKone, Zainab Zahra, Pao Pongsala; Capstone Project (\*Innovation and Willingness to Take Risks Award)

### Overview

Researched and designed a hybrid solution for an educational and collaborative extended reality (XR) space. The project is sponsored by STMicroelectronics and the Department of Human Centered Design & Engineering with an aim to explore inclusive and accessible XR system to increase XR product demands in the long term.
